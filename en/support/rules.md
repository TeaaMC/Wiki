# Server rules

A few rules to keep TeaaMC fair and pleasant for everyone. Joining the server means you agree to follow them — not knowing a rule isn't an excuse.

## Client & mod rules

- No **hacked clients** or **ghost clients**
- No **inventory mods**
- No **mouse tweaks mods**
- No **radar / freecam / auto-place**
- No mods that **assist or interfere with gameplay** in any way
- No **illegal macros**

## Gameplay rules

- Don't abuse server **bugs or exploits**
- No item **duping**
- No **real-money trading (RMT)** or cross-trading
- No **impersonation**
- No **punishment evasion** (alt accounts to dodge a ban, etc.)

## Chat rules (Discord & in-game)

- No **spam** — sending the same thing repeatedly
- No **insults, discrimination or harassment**
- Don't **share anyone's personal information**
- No **advertising**
- Don't **encourage self-harm**
- Don't **bypass or try to get around the chat filter**

::: warning Breaking the rules has consequences
Depending on severity, violations may lead to a warning, mute, kick or ban. The staff team has full discretion to apply the punishment that fits each case.
:::
