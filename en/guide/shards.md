# Shards

Shards are TeaaMC's currency. You earn them by playing — fighting, joining Crystal Royale, or even just idling AFK — and spend them on [cosmetics and tags](/en/guide/cosmetics) in the shop.

## How to earn shards

| How | Reward |
|---|---|
| Killing a player (regular PvP) | **1 shard** per kill |
| A kill in Crystal Royale | **10 shards** per kill |
| Winning a Crystal Royale event | **200 shards** |
| Idling AFK | **1 shard** every 3 minutes |

In other words, Crystal Royale is the fastest way to stack shards — each kill is worth ten times a regular PvP kill, and winning the whole match nets a flat 200 shards.

## What to spend them on

All shards are spent in the cosmetics shop. Run `/shop` to buy effects (sprint, armor, arrow, kill, elytra), pets and tags — see the [Cosmetics & Tag](/en/guide/cosmetics) page for the details.

::: tip Farm shards while you're away
No time to play actively? Just leave your character AFK — you'll still tick up 1 shard every 3 minutes without doing anything.
:::
